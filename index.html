What this is

A rectangle on casters. Each shelf has its own pair of rods — a left jaw and a right jaw — then a dedicated air spacer before the next pair. Faces cannot kiss. Front and back rails are the cradle. The back rail is the stop. Discs stand vertical on the short span so they do not sink.

Shelf

Rack

13 in is 1 in under the spoken 14 in pinch. Extra width is extra track — that is the stability you can actually buy. Do not go to 14.

Keep this near 13 in. That hangs the disc 1.85 in below the rails — not 5 in. An 18 in cart (Mike’s video) is the 5 in sink, and the disc hits the floor.

Two rods per shelf, then a spacer. 1/4 in jaws leave more air between faces. 3/8 in if you want stiffer rods.

2.75 in overall, 2 in phenolic wheel. Not a 3 in furniture caster on a 2½ in plate — that plate and the swivel swing into the shelves. See the review below.
